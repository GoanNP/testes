# Robotics Cell

A pre-integrated workstation for pick, place and palletizing tasks at the end
of a production line.

## Highlights

- Six-axis arm with 12 kg payload and 1.4 m reach.
- Tool changer supporting vacuum, mechanical and magnetic grippers.
- Recipe-driven layer patterns for standard EUR pallets.

## Cycle data

| Task | Cycle time | Accuracy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pick and place | 1.6 s | ±0.05 mm |
| Carton palletizing | 3.4 s | ±1.0 mm |
| Tray loading | 2.1 s | ±0.2 mm |

## Commissioning

1. Anchor the cell frame and connect 24 V DC plus compressed air.
2. Import the pallet pattern file exported from the plant ERP.
3. Teach the three reference points of the infeed conveyor.
4. Run the dry cycle without product for 30 minutes.

## Safety

The cell ships with a fenceless scanner package. The scanner zones must be
validated by the machine integrator before production use.
